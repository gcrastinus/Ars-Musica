## The new study question

On i-1, after the question on delight. The true option is the second (`a` is 1). It is shorter than the first option.

Question: “Arithmetic may state an application to geometry without proving that application in detail. What does the same relation come to when music speaks of ethics?”

- “Music must prove every such application in full, or it may not mention ethics, politics, or cosmology.” Response: stating and demonstrating are two different acts.
- “Music may state an application to ethics without proving it as a conclusion.” Response: as arithmetic may state an application to geometry without proving it in detail, so music may state an application to ethics, politics, or cosmology without proving it as a conclusion. The demonstration belongs to the other science.
- “Music proves the application by the same demonstration that shows the fifth to be 3:2.” Response: a fifth is proved in harmonics; an application to ethics is stated here.
- “Ethics proves the ratios of the string, and music proves what a city ought to sing.” Response: the parallel runs the other way.
