# Plan: music states the application

Governing sentence (Timothy’s wording, kept). It will stand in two places, because they are different pages. Lesson i-1 is where the course first raises applications of music to ethics and politics. The contemplation c-ethos is the section on character, and a student can open it without having that lesson in front of him. The contemplations can also be skipped, so the sentence has to stand in the lesson as well.

> Music here can state what has been discovered for applications of music in other arts and sciences, but in those cases music theory itself is primarily stating the application, not proving that it is true, since the demonstrations belong properly to the other sciences. Just as in arithmetic we might state some of the science’s applications to geometry without proving them in detail, so too in music we might state some of the applications of music to ethics, politics, or even cosmology, but without proving them as conclusions.

No new material from Saint Thomas. Right-answer indexes stay as they are. Historical uses of “reports” (an author reports a fact, a lesson reports a discrepancy in the numbers) stay.
